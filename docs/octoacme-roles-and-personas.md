# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide business sponsorship, prioritize funding and strategic alignment, and support escalation decisions when trade-offs or resource constraints need executive input.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business case and expected value of the initiative
- Sponsor scope, timeline, and resource decisions at the executive level
- Resolve escalations involving priority, budget, or organizational dependencies
- Review major milestones, outcomes, and risks with the PM and Product Manager

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with the Product Manager to validate strategic alignment and success measures
- Partners with the Project Manager to receive high-level status and escalate cross-team blockers
- Gives direction to development leadership when business priorities shift or additional support is required
- Helps ensure the team has visible sponsorship and accountability for delivery outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone reviews, and risk escalations
- Business case and outcome reviews
- Executive decisions when projects need rescoping or prioritization changes

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect business needs, process flows, and operational realities to project delivery. They help make requirements clear and ensure that work matches stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit requirements and document workflows, pain points, and constraints
- Translate business needs into clear user stories, process maps, and acceptance criteria
- Clarify ambiguous requirements for Product Managers, Developers, and QA
- Support change impact analysis and validation of outcomes after delivery

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partners with the Product Manager on problem statements, priorities, and backlog clarity
- Works with Developers and QA to ensure requirements are testable and implementation matches intent
- Coordinates with the Project Manager to surface dependencies, stakeholder impacts, and readiness concerns
- Helps maintain alignment between business needs and delivery changes throughout the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and backlog refinement sessions
- Acceptance-criteria discussions and change-impact reviews
- Stakeholder alignment updates during planning and release preparation

---

## UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers ensure the project delivers usable, accessible, and customer-centered outcomes. They ground decisions in user needs and design quality.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability checks
- Define user journeys, interaction flows, and design requirements
- Partner with Product Managers to prioritize customer experience improvements
- Validate that implementation matches accessibility and usability expectations

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer problems and desired experience outcomes
- Collaborates with Developers on feasibility, interaction design, and edge cases
- Partners with QA to review usability, accessibility, and acceptance criteria
- Supports the Project Manager by identifying design dependencies or stakeholder review needs

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design reviews
- Prototype and usability feedback sessions
- Design signoff and iteration checkpoints throughout delivery

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, standards, and architecture decisions so the team can deliver reliable and scalable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, integration patterns, and design constraints
- Review implementation approaches and identify technical risks early
- Guide engineering decisions for scalability, maintainability, and security
- Coordinate complex dependencies across teams or systems

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review design quality, technical trade-offs, and implementation guidance
- Partners with the Product Manager on feasibility and sequencing of roadmap items
- Supports Project Managers in identifying cross-team dependencies and technical risk impacts
- Coordinates with Security, QA, and Operations partners to ensure readiness for release and support

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Dependency and risk reviews
- Technical planning and release-readiness checkpoints

---

## Operations / Release or Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and Release engineers help ensure solutions are reliable, observable, and safe to deploy. They focus on production readiness, monitoring, and recovery procedures.

### Responsibilities
- Validate deployment readiness, rollback plans, and operational controls
- Define monitoring, alerting, and on-call needs for new features or services
- Support smoke testing and post-deployment verification
- Participate in incident response and service recovery planning

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partners with Developers and QA before and after release to validate operational readiness
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on release timing, communication, and rollback decisions
- Works with Product Managers to confirm reliability impacts and support expectations
- Helps Technical Leads and Stakeholders understand production risk, monitoring gaps, and operability trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Post-deploy verification and incident follow-up
- Operational dashboards and service health reports

---

## Security / Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy partners help the team protect customer data, comply with requirements, and reduce risk throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and privacy requirements during planning and design
- Identify risks, control gaps, and mitigation steps for new functionality
- Support secure coding, testing, and access management practices
- Validate readiness before release or handoff to production

### How they interact with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk, compliance, and stakeholder expectations
- Collaborates with Technical Leads and Developers on secure design patterns and required controls
- Works with QA to validate test coverage for privacy, data handling, and security requirements
- Helps Operations and Release teams understand go-live criteria and incident response needs

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, security signoff checkpoints, and compliance discussions
- Design and implementation guidance for sensitive systems or data
- Release gating and remediation planning for identified issues

---

## Change Management / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Change Management and Support representatives help teams prepare for adoption, training, and operational continuity when a project introduces process or product changes.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare support teams, users, and stakeholders for rollout and adoption
- Clarify training needs, process changes, and launch communications
- Gather feedback after deployment and identify operational friction
- Help capture lessons learned and experience-data to improve future changes

### How they interact with existing roles
- Coordinates with the Product Manager on launch readiness and communication priorities
- Works with the Project Manager to align timing, readiness, and stakeholder updates
- Partners with Developers and Operations to understand expected user impact and support escalations
- Helps QA and Product teams validate that the user experience and support model are ready for production use

### Typical Communication
- Readiness checklists, launch briefings, and support handoff updates
- Feedback loops during rollout and post-release stabilization
- Stakeholder and support communications before and after adoption

---

## Data / Analytics Partner

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics partners help teams define measurement, validation, and reporting so product or process decisions are informed by evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation, dashboards, and success metrics for the initiative
- Validate reportability of outcomes, trends, and product performance
- Support analysis of experiments, milestones, and operational health
- Help teams connect project outcomes to customer and business impact

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate goals into measurable outcomes and reporting strategy
- Partners with Developers and Technical Leads to ensure the right events and metrics are available
- Supports Project Managers and Sponsors with milestone and performance reporting
- Helps QA and Operations confirm that the release meets expected quality and usage outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions, dashboard reviews, and outcome reporting
- Post-release analysis and stakeholder updates
- Inputs for retrospectives and continuous improvement planning

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a project is small, multiple roles may be combined, but the responsibilities and handoffs above still help clarify accountability and decision ownership.
